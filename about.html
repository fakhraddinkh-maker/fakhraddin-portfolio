About Me

PhD researcher combining animal science expertise with modern bioinformatics approaches.

Professional Summary

I am a PhD researcher in Animal Science with a background in poultry nutrition, molecular biology and biotechnology. My current research interests focus on transcriptomics, RNA-seq analysis, functional genomics and computational approaches for biological data interpretation.

Through my research experience, I have worked on animal production, microbial biotechnology, enzyme research and molecular analysis. Recently, I have developed reproducible R-based RNA-seq workflows for gene expression analysis, functional enrichment and biological network investigation.
