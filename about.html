Researcher Profile

Dr. Fakhraddin Khodayari is an Animal Science researcher with a background in poultry science, animal nutrition, molecular biology and applied livestock research.

His current research interests focus on poultry genomics, chicken transcriptomics, RNA-seq analysis, functional genomics and bioinformatics, with particular interest in broiler breeder research and reproductive performance.

His research combines biological knowledge from animal science with computational approaches for analysing gene expression, identifying differentially expressed genes and interpreting molecular pathways.

Academic Background

Dr. Khodayari received his PhD in Animal Science from Çukurova University, Türkiye.

His academic background includes animal nutrition, poultry science, reproductive biology, molecular genetics and applied animal production.

This multidisciplinary background provides a biological foundation for interpreting high-throughput genomic and transcriptomic datasets.

Poultry Genomics and RNA-seq

Current research development focuses on the application of RNA-seq and functional genomics to poultry research.

  • RNA-seq transcriptomic analysis
  • Differential gene expression analysis
  • DESeq2 statistical analysis
  • Gene annotation and functional interpretation
  • Gene Ontology enrichment
  • KEGG pathway analysis
  • Gene Set Enrichment Analysis (GSEA)
  • Protein-protein interaction network analysis
  • Functional genomics of broiler breeder chickens

Research Approach

The research approach combines animal science and biological knowledge with reproducible computational workflows.

The objective is to transform high-throughput molecular data into biologically meaningful results that can contribute to poultry breeding, nutrition, reproductive research and precision animal science.

Current Bioinformatics Development

Current computational work includes development of reproducible R-based workflows for RNA-seq analysis, including quality control, differential expression, functional enrichment, pathway analysis and network interpretation.

A major research project focuses on transcriptomic responses in broiler breeder chickens under different feeding conditions.
